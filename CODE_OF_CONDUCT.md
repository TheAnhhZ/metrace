# Code of Conduct

We want metrace to be a friendly place to contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public channels (issues, pull requests).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block contributors who
violate this code. Report problems privately to the maintainers listed in
`AUTHORS` (via their GitHub profile).

This text is our own short summary. For a fuller community standard we
recommend reading the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
